1. Revenue Sharing
On every eSIM purchase, half of RoamFi's net margin (after subtracting the provider cost) doesn't go to the company — it goes into a shared pool that's automatically distributed to earlier buyers. Example: a $10 purchase with a $5 cost price leaves a $5 margin → $2.5 stays with the company, $2.5 goes into the distribution pool. This isn't a referral bonus — it's built into every transaction.
Anyone who has purchased an eSIM from RoamFi within the last 365 days. Your share doesn't depend on who referred whom. Distribution happens across all active buyers, weighted by which of the 4 time windows they fall into.
No, participation is fully automatic. Simply buying an eSIM enters you into the pool for 365 days — no link-sharing, tracking, or separate account is required.
Instantly, right after every purchase made by any user on the platform — not once a day or once a month. Your balance in the RoamFi app updates in real time.
The pool is split into 4 windows based on how recently a buyer purchased, relative to the moment of a new sale: 40% of the pool goes to buyers from the last 24 hours, 30% to buyers from 1–7 days ago, 20% to buyers from 8–30 days ago, and 10% to buyers from 31–365 days ago. The more recent the purchase, the higher the weight, but eligibility lasts the full 365 days.
A purchase from 17 days ago falls into the "8–30 days" window, which receives 20% of the pool on every new sale. The exact amount depends on how many other people are also in that window at the time of the sale — the 20% is split among all of them, not paid out to one person.
Within a window, the payout is split in proportion to how much each person spent on their purchases in that window — not split evenly. Example: 5 people are in the "8–30 days" window, having spent $100 combined, and $20 of that was your purchase → you get 20% of that window's 20% pool share (i.e., 1/5 of the amount allocated to that window).
Since money from the Revenue Sharing Pool is credited directly to your account's USD balance, you can either spend it within RoamFi or withdraw it to fiat or crypto from the app. No additional KYC is required beyond standard email registration.
